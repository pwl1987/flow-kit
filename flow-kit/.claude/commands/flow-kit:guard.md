读取并执行护栏管理操作：

@flow-kit/commands/careful.md

用法：

- `/flow-kit:guard full` — 棕地: B1-B6 全开
- `/flow-kit:guard minimal` — 绿地: 核心护栏 (B2/B4/B6)
- `/flow-kit:guard enable B2` — 单独启用指定护栏
- `/flow-kit:guard disable B5` — 单独禁用指定护栏
